**Phase 4 verdict: PASS**
- A_min_plateau (δ≤0.01) = 3.0799e-05 ≈ A_ref = 3.0801e-05 (99.99%)
- |ε(δ)| ∝ δ → 0 **linearly** — Prize limit is REGULAR, not singular
- The μ(T)→ν transition does NOT collapse the LPS margin
- A_min never approaches 0; it approaches A_ref from below

**Physical interpretation:**
Cold spots in the initial T field have slightly lower A(T) than A(T̄), giving A_min < A_ref.
As δ→0, those cold spots vanish and A_min→A_ref monotonically.
The deviation is first-order: |ε(δ)| ≈ c·δ where c ∝ |A'(T̄)|.
This is exactly what Phase 4 requires: the limit is smooth and non-singular.
