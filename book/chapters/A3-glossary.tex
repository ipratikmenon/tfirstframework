\chapter{Glossary of Symbols and Named Objects}
\label{ch:glossary}

\begin{record}
Every symbol and named object used in this book, with the place it is defined
in the proof document where one exists. Line numbers are as of \Sn{48} and are
secondary; labels are primary and stable.

\smallskip
Two conventions recur and are worth stating here rather than repeating. A
\emph{hypothesis} in bold parentheses --- \textbf{(R)}, \textbf{(N)},
\textbf{(A-up)} --- is an assumption carried explicitly in a statement's
hypothesis list. A \texttt{conj:} or \texttt{target:} label is an
\OPEN{} statement the programme would like to prove and has not.
\end{record}
